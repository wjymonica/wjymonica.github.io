Jingying Wang

Hi, I am Jingying Wang, a Ph.D. candidate from the Computer Science and Engineering department of the University of Michigan. I am co-advised by Prof. Xu Wang from the UM CSE, and Vitaliy Popov from UM Medicine.

My research lies at the intersection of Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) and medical education. I develop human-ai system for externalizing surgical expertise including perceptual cognitive skill, psychomotor skill, and attentional skill. My projects includes systems include multimodal sensing in OR, XR and computer vision, and interactive learning.

Research & Publications

CueOnGaze

Paper in submission

A gaze-guided learning system that compares a trainee’s actions and visual attention with an expert’s recorded action–attention template. When gaze is misaligned with the next task, the system provides audio and visual cues to guide attention to the relevant object.

Mentored by Jun Rekimoto.
